==================== EDA EXPLANATION ====================

1. Rating Distribution
   - Most courses are rated between 4 and 5
   - Indicates generally positive user feedback

2. Duration Distribution
   - Most courses are short in duration
   - Few courses are very long
   - This right-skewed pattern is common in real-world datasets

3. Scatter Plots
   - Each dot represents one course
   - Used to analyze relationships between variables

4. Rating vs Review Count
   - X-axis uses log scale of review count
   - Log scale compresses large values (e.g., 10 to 100000)
   - Helps visualize data more clearly
   - Slight positive trend observed:
     More popular courses tend to have slightly higher ratings

5. Rating vs Duration
   - No strong relationship observed
   - Course length does not significantly affect ratings

6. Dot Shading (Transparency)
   - Darker regions → many overlapping points (high density)
   - Lighter regions → fewer data points
   - Helps identify where most data is concentrated

7. Correlation Heatmap
   - Shows relationships between numerical variables
   - All correlations are low (close to 0)
   - Indicates variables are mostly independent

-------------------- FINAL CONCLUSION --------------------

- Data shows realistic patterns similar to real-world platforms
- Ratings are mostly high (4–5 range)
- Duration follows a right-skewed distribution
- Relationships between variables are weak but logical
- Dataset is clean and suitable for further analysis
